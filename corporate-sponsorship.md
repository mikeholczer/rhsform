## We need your help!

Our school takes great pride in its vibrant and talented music program, which enriches the lives of students and strengthens our community. Over 200 students participate in our choirs, bands, orchestras, and marching band. We need your help to ensure these students continue to thrive.

Music education provides students with far more than just the ability to play an instrument or sing in a choir — it fosters critical thinking, teamwork, discipline, personal expression, and creativity. Most importantly, our music program serves a key role in shaping our community at Rockville High School.

However, running a successful music program requires significant resources. Unfortunately, like many public schools, our music department faces budget constraints that limit access to the necessary instruments, sheet music, technology, and performance opportunities. For the 2025-26 school year in particular, the music department requires a new speaker system — at a cost of $12,000. This is where we hope you can help.

Your sponsorship will directly benefit the music department at Rockville High School. In return, your company will receive significant exposure to audiences at our students' concerts and performances, music competitions, football games, and more — all throughout the school year. FORM is a charitable organization, and we will be happy to provide you with our Tax ID number for your records.

This is a wonderful opportunity to grow your business while enriching your community — and to support our students' dreams in the process. Your generosity would make a lasting difference in the lives of our students, our music program, and our community.

Sponsorship plans are for one school year and subsequent summer, September through September of the following year.
