## Who are Friends of Rockville Music (FORM)?

We are an all-volunteer, private, charitable 501(c)(3) organization that supports the outstanding Instrumental, Choral and Band programs that comprise the Music Department at Rockville High School. We also help support the Music Department at Earle B. Wood Middle School via our annual cluster concerts.

**What do we do?** We provide the people power &amp; the monetary support to help make the RHS Music community strong!

We want to see all families with music students at Rockville High School to become members and get involved! We also encourage all RHS cluster parents and community members to become members, as well—connect with &amp; help build your student's future music community now!

If you have any questions about FORM or wish to volunteer, email [musicrhs@gmail.com](mailto:musicrhs@gmail.com).

We will do our best to reply within 24-48 hours.
