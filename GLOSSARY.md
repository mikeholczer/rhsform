# Friends of Rockville Music (FORM)

FORM is the volunteer nonprofit that funds and organizes support for the Music Department at Rockville High School (RHS), and — through cluster concerts — the music program at Earle B. Wood Middle School. This site is the public face of that work.

## Language

**FORM**:
Friends of Rockville Music, the all-volunteer 501(c)(3) that raises money and organizes volunteers for the RHS Music Department.
_Avoid_: The Foundation, Booster Club, F.O.R.M.

**Music Department**:
The combined instrumental, choral, theatre, band, and orchestra programs at Rockville High School that FORM supports.
_Avoid_: Music Program (reserve for a single ensemble)

**Marching Band**:
The RHS Rams marching band, which performs at home football games and competitions and is a major focus of FORM fundraising.
_Avoid_: The band (ambiguous with concert band / jazz band)

**Spirit Store**:
FORM's print-on-demand merchandise shop, hosted on Printify, selling apparel and accessories (t-shirts, hoodies, blankets, mugs).
_Avoid_: Merch store, Gear shop

**Corporate Sponsorship**:
A paid partnership with a local business for one school year (September through the following September), offered in three tiers: Platinum ($1,200), Gold ($700), and Silver ($350).
_Avoid_: Donation, Advertising (a sponsorship buys named benefits; a donation does not)

**Flocking**:
A fundraiser in which volunteers briefly place a "flock" of ten orange-and-black flamingos in a supporter's yard.
_Avoid_: Flamingo drive

**Cluster Concert**:
A joint performance that raises support for the Music Department at Earle B. Wood Middle School.
_Avoid_: Feeder concert

**Instrument Truck**:
The trailer that carries the Marching Band's instruments to competitions and displays sponsor banners.
_Avoid_: Equipment trailer
